The kerning is the message

A short field guide to the space between letters, and why professionals lose sleep over it.

Caleb Yoon
· 22 Jul 2026 · 1 min read
On this page

    Kerning is the space between individual letters, and caring about it is one of the surest signs that someone has crossed from using type into setting it. The amateur adjusts the font. The professional adjusts the gaps.

    Once you see bad kerning you cannot unsee it. I am sorry to have done this to you.
