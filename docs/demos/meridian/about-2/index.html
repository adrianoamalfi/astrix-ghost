About

Meridian is an independent travel journal. We publish slow, first-person dispatches from specific places — a harbour at blue hour, the last ferry of the season, the exact bend where the tarmac gives out — and the photographs that come back with them.

No listicles, no top-tens. Just the road, and what it was actually like to be there. New pieces land when the light is worth it.
