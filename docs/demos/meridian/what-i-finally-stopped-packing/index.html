What I pack, and what I finally stopped packing

Eleven years of over-packing, distilled into a short list and one hard-won rule.

Sana Okafor
· 22 Jul 2026 · 1 min read
On this page

    I used to travel with a bag that could survive a siege. Now I travel with one that can survive a long walk, which turns out to be the thing that actually happens.

    What earns its place

    • One jacket that is warm when dry and useless when wet, plus one that is the opposite
    • A notebook, because the phone is a terrible place to think
    • Two of exactly one thing: socks

    What I stopped bringing

    • The second camera body I have used precisely twice
    • Books I intend to read rather than books I am reading
    • Anything justified by the sentence "just in case"

    The rule underneath all of it: if I cannot carry it up a hill at the end of a long day, it is not coming.
